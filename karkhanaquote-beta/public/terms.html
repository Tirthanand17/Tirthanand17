Beta terms

KarkhanaQuote Beta is an experimental costing and quotation aid. It is not accounting, tax, engineering or legal advice.

Verify before use

Fabrication pricing depends on machine capability, sheet utilization, consumables, labour, minimum charges, finishing, logistics, taxes and other shop-specific factors. You are responsible for reviewing every figure before using it in a commercial quotation.

No production guarantee

The calculator uses the values entered by the operator. It does not validate manufacturability, drawing accuracy, nesting efficiency, machine speed, material availability or final production cost.

Beta availability

The beta may change. Export important quote records before clearing browser data or changing devices.
