Privacy notice

KarkhanaQuote Beta is designed as an offline-first browser tool. Quote details, customer names, workshop settings and saved quote history are stored in your browser's local storage on the device you use.

What this beta does not do

The current beta does not create user accounts, sync quotes to a central database, or intentionally transmit quote data to KarkhanaQuote servers.

Your responsibility

Anyone with access to your browser profile or device may be able to access locally stored quotation data. Do not store information you are not authorized to keep. Clearing browser site data may permanently delete saved quotes.

Future versions

If cloud accounts, analytics, payments or email features are added later, this notice must be updated before those features are enabled.
