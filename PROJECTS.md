### 5. People Detection & Tracking
**Repository:** [Tirthanand17/People-Detector-and-uniqe-id](https://github.com/Tirthanand17/People-Detector-and-uniqe-id)

A compact computer-vision prototype using a pretrained Faster R-CNN model and simple centroid-distance association to assign IDs across video frames.

**Skills:** PyTorch · torchvision · Faster R-CNN · OpenCV · NumPy · video processing

**Current scope:** educational/prototype tracking rather than a production multi-object tracker. The simple distance-based association is intentionally easy to inspect and understand.
